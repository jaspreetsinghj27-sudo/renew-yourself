# Visual requirements
- Light-dominant palette per the 90/8/2 spec: warm off-white #F7F5F0 canvas throughout, deep charcoal #111111 reserved for the fixed nav bar, footer, headings and body copy, soft warm gray #E8E5DE for borders/surfaces.
- Muted electric blue #3157FF is a ~2% accent only: primary CTAs, inline links, hover states, focus rings, and thin micro-accents (hairline underlines on key phrases, eyebrow dashes, the build-cursor/progress and before/after sweep). Never large blue surface areas.
- Never use pure white #FFFFFF or pure black #000000; the warm off-white and deep charcoal replace them everywhere.
- Keep the moving website-build browser mockup, finished website previews, and before/after redesign comparisons.
- Keep the portfolio premium, editorial, restrained, and conversion-focused.
